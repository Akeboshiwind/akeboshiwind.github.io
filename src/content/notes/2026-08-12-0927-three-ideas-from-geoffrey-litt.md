Ideas I'm taking from from Geoffrey Litt's [Understanding is the new bottleneck](https://www.geoffreylitt.com/2026/07/02/understanding-is-the-new-bottleneck.html):

# Better explanations
The explanations Claude gives me are fine, but it takes a lot of effort to get that understanding into my head. This effort can be brought down with diagrams and interactivity. HTML is great for this, he has a [skill](https://gist.github.com/geoffreylitt/a29df1b5f9865506e8952488eac3d524) he uses.

# Quizzes
One of the best ways to check (and force) understanding is to try answering some questions. Just make sure doesn't make it obvious the answer is the longest with the bold title "👉 load-bearing".
