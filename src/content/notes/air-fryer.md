Today my wife turned on the Air Fryer with *nothing in it*. It didn't reject that as invalid input. It didn't throw an error. It turned on, as normal.

[Our unit](https://www.sharkninja.co.uk/ninja-crispi-4-in-1-portable-glass-air-fryer-white-tote-bag-fn102uk/FN102UK.html) doesn't have a pre-heat setting, so I assumed it was impossible and made mediocre hash browns. But an Air Fryer is a simple tool, it turns on a heated coil that can be used in a variety of ways. Including pre-heating.

I should remember that for other tools more often, and imagine [software that works that way too](https://www.inkandswitch.com/malleable-software/).
